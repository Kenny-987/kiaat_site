    About Kiaat Technologies

    We are an MSP (managed service provider), but our services are more direct and focused on a very specific challenge. Unlike most MSP who cover a borad range such as IT and helpdesk we focus on helping businesses build dependable, independent infrastructure through VPS hosting and self-hosted environments. We believe businesses should have as much ownership, controll and privacy over their infrastructure and data as they can all at affordable and manageable costs without worrying about hardware overheads.. We know that implementing this type of infrastructure can quickly become overwhelming, needs specialization which some businesses are not prepared to get from a full time employee. Our role is to make that process feel approachable, reliable, well supported and most importantly affordable

    Why We Exist

    We started with a simple belief: people should have more control over the technology they rely on every day. Too many platforms push businesses into systems that are expensive, restrictive, or difficult to move away from. You should never be punished for growing you business. Self-hosting and VPS infrastructure offer freedom and flexibility, but they often come with a steep learning curve.
    We exist to bridge that gap, helping clients build infrastructure that is independent, secure, and designed around their actual needs while they focus on running thier businesses.

    Our Values & Beliefs

    • Pivacy: Business should have maximum privacy and have control over who has access to their data
    • Ownership: We believe businesses should own their infrastructure and be fully flexible in how they want to it to be. We make it as easy as possible for businesses, should they decide to switch MSPs or get an internal admin, to move with proper documentation and process guidelines.
    • Control: No infrastructure should have control on how a business is run, we beleive you should be in control of every aspect of your infrastructure.
    • Reliability: We believe stable systems and dependable support matter more than trends or unnecessary complexity.

    Why Work With Us

    We understand that not everyone wants a complicated enterprise solution. Many businesses simply need dependable infrastructure, clear communication, and support they can trust.
    That’s why we focus on:

    • Clean, stable server environments
    • Security-first configurations
    • Honest recommendations
    • Responsive support
    • Solutions tailored to your actual needs

    Whether you are self-hosting business tools, deploying client applications, or managing growing workloads, we aim to provide infrastructure that feels dependable and and we handle all the heavy lifting.
