    Managed IT & Support

    Your IT Team, Without the Overhead.

    Most small and medium businesses can't justify a full-time IT person — but they still need one. We act as your on-call IT team, handling everything from day-to-day troubleshooting and hardware issues to software setup and device management. When something goes wrong, you have someone to call who actually knows your setup.

    Why have managed IT support?

    Technology problems don't wait for a convenient time. A slow computer, a failed update, a printer that won't connect — small issues that eat into your team's productive time every day. Having dedicated IT support means those problems get resolved fast, and most of the time we catch them before you even notice.

    Helpdesk & Troubleshooting Fast, reliable support when your team hits a technical problem.

    Hardware Repairs & Upgrades We assess, repair and upgrade devices so your team always has working tools.

    Software Setup & Licensing The right software, installed correctly, on every dev

    Proactive Monitoring We watch your systems so issues are caught and fixed before they cause disruption.

    Onboarding & Offboarding New staff member or someone leaving — we handle the tech setup and access management.

    One Point of Contact No call centres, no ticket queues. You deal with people who know your business.
