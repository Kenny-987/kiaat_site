    Data Migration and Backup

    Your data, moved safely and backed up properly

    When managing a server, backups are one of the most important things to do. We handle all the backup gruntwork, automation and restoration. Disasters happen uninvited most of the time and we always ensure we a re prepared for the worst case scenario, operations can go smoothly 6 months in a row, but unexpected things can happen unexpected and we are always prepared. If you decide to change servers, switch msp's your data will be save and available.

    Why back up your data?

    Most people only think about backups after something goes wrong. A backup plan means that whether it's accidental deletion, a hardware fault, or a security incident, your files and data can be recovered, fast, and without starting from scratch.
