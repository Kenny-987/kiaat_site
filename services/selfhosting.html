    Self-Hosted Apps

    Your favourite apps, running on your own server

    Self-hosting means running the software you rely on yourself, on your own server, rather than depending on a third-party company to do it for you. It gives you full control over your data, no monthly subscription fees, and the freedom to customise things to suit how you actually work. Whether it's a project management tool, a file storage platform, a password manager, or something else entirely, we install it, configure it, and make sure it's secure and accessible to whoever needs it. You get all the benefits of the software without handing your data over to someone else's servers.

    Why self-host your apps?

    Subscription costs add up, and with cloud services you're always at the mercy of price hikes, feature changes, or a company shutting down. Self-hosting puts you back in control, your apps, your data, your rules.

    No subscription feesPay once for your server and run your apps for free, rather than paying monthly forever.

    Your data stays yoursNothing is stored on someone else's servers or shared with third parties.

    Fully customisableRConfigure the app exactly how you want it, without being locked into what a vendor allows.

    Add as many users as you likeNo per-seat pricing. Add your whole team without the bill going up.

    Fast and private Running on your own server often means better performance and no ads or tracking.

    Wide range of appsFrom file storage and note-taking to CRMs and communication tools, most popular apps have a self-hosted version.
